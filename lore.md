# Pop Brigade — The Reefweavers

> **One-line pitch.** A dying coral reef is being drained of color by The Bleach. You are the youngest Reefweaver — only your kind can pop the spirit-bubbles that hold the reef's lost heroes and call them back to defend what's left of the color.

The theme is a deliberate hybrid: **Tide Pool's cozy-marine wrapper** (white-space aesthetic for the female-skew hybrid-casual audience) + **Bubble Realms' faction-color architecture** (load-bearing live-ops scaffold). It deliberately abandons two earlier moves: the eco-villain framing (preachy, 2018) and the high-fantasy "Realm Hearts / Void Foam" cosmology (most saturated genre in mobile gaming).

---

## 1. The world (cosmology, 4 sentences)

The world is the Great Reef — a sprawling underwater kingdom whose magic *is* color itself. Long ago, the reef's heroes ascended into floating spirit-bubbles that drift above the coral, ready to be called back when the reef needs them. But a sickness called **The Bleach** is spreading: a colorless rot that drains the chroma from coral, fish, and finally from people, leaving everything dead-white and still. Only the Reefweavers, a near-extinct line chosen by the Tidemother, can pop the spirit-bubbles and bring the heroes — and the color — back.

---

## 2. The player

You are **a young Reefweaver**, the last to inherit the gift. You have a launcher-shell — a conch staff that fires water-pearls up into the spirit-bubble cluster overhead. You're not a fighter. You're the one who calls the fighters.

This solves the protagonist problem the agents flagged: the player has a *named role* (Reefweaver) with a *visible function* (popper of bubbles) and a *clear stake* (the reef is dying). Not a vague "Commander."

---

## 3. The five factions (bubble color → faction → biome → archetype)

The single best idea from the Bubble Realms proposal — faction-color mapping — is the spine. The five existing bubble colors get one faction each.

| Color | Faction | Biome | Hero archetype | Vibe |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Red | **Coral Court** | Shallow coral reef | Knight (Fire Knight → Coral Knight) | Vibrant red living coral, regal, warm-water royalty |
| 🔵 Blue | **Tide Mystics** | Open-ocean current | Mage (Ice Mage → Tide Mage) | Deep cold blue, hooded, current-summoners |
| 🟡 Yellow | **Sunshoal** | Sunlit shallow shore | Archer | Golden, sand-and-sun, fast and agile |
| 🟢 Green | **Kelp Wardens** | Kelp forest / mangrove | Druid | Mossy green, gentle giants, plant-callers |
| 🟣 Purple | **Abyssal Order** | Bioluminescent deep | Wizard | Glowing violet, anglerfish-lure staffs, deep-magic |

Each faction has a unique aesthetic, a unique signature ability flavor (Coral = block/tank, Tide = AoE wave, Sunshoal = pierce, Kelp = entangle/slow, Abyssal = burst nuke), and a unique recruitment-bubble color. Late meta: faction synergies, faction-vs-faction events, faction skins.

**Why this matters:** This is the architecture that lets you ship 12 months of live ops without thrashing. Banners rotate by faction. Realms unlock by biome. Skins and lore drop per faction. The Bubble Realms author was right that this scaffolding is genuinely strong — it just needed a fresh wrapper.

---

## 4. The antagonist — The Bleach

A coral reef dying of bleaching is real, harrowing, and — reframed as a fantasy threat — visually devastating. The Bleach is not a villain with a plan. It's a *condition*. Things touched by it lose their color first, then their voice, then their movement.

**The mob roster:**
- **Bleached Crawlers** — chibi dead-coral chunks with sad white button eyes. Walk slow, bite hard.
- **Pale Eels** — ghost-eels that strip color from heroes mid-fight (debuff mechanic).
- **The Hollowmouth** (mini-boss) — a bleached-out angler with a dead lure. Drains adjacent enemy color to heal.
- **The Whitening King** (realm 1 boss) — a colossal bleached parrotfish-king. Strips the lane's color while it lives.

**Why this works:**
- **Visual contrast = mechanic legibility.** The world is saturated; The Bleach is stark white-gray. At a glance the player knows what's a hero and what's a threat. Same trick Brawl Stars uses with its yellow-and-black.
- **Mascot-friendly.** A chibi Bleached Crawler with sad button eyes plushifies day one. So does the Hollowmouth.
- **Thematic spine.** The antagonist is *literally* the absence of color in a game whose entire mechanic is built on color. Every bubble you pop, every hero you summon, every wave you clear is the reef *coming back to life*. That's a real emotional throughline, not decoration.
- **Not preachy.** Bleaching exists in real reefs but the framing here is mythic, not advocacy. The eco-villain trap is avoided.

## 5. Mechanic-to-fiction map (every mechanic gets a fiction reason)

| Mechanic | Fiction |
|---|---|
| Bubble cluster overhead | Spirit-bubbles drifting above the reef where heroes wait |
| Pop a bubble | Call a hero back to the reef |
| Hero bubbles (special) | Named spirits — your collected roster |
| Non-hero bubbles | Pearl-bubbles. Pop them to clear the path to a hero. |
| Bigger match = higher tier | A stronger weave wakes a stronger hero |
| Lane | The reef floor below the spirit-cloud |
| Enemy wave | The Bleach advancing on the reef |
| Merge (B+B→S→G) | Two heroes of the same faction "weave together" — their color deepens, their shell hardens |
| Tier visual | Bronze = pale-shell hero, Silver = full-color hero, Gold = crowned-coral hero with a glowing aura |
| Realm progression | Diving deeper — Shore → Reef → Kelp → Trench → Abyss |
| Gacha pull | The Tidemother grants you a new spirit-bubble |
| Daily energy | Tides — you can only weave so many times before the current weakens |
| Guild | Reefweaver Coven |

Nothing is arbitrary. Every existing mechanic in `concept.md` has a fiction-reason in this theme.

## 6. Visual identity

**Palette (the game's signature):**
- `#FF4D6D` coral red — Coral Court
- `#1E6091` tide blue — Tide Mystics
- `#FFD166` sun yellow — Sunshoal
- `#2A9D8F` kelp teal — Kelp Wardens
- `#9D4EDD` abyss violet — Abyssal Order
- `#F1FAEE` bleach white — The antagonist
- `#0B132B` deep-trench navy — backgrounds, contrast

**Signature silhouette motifs:**
- Every hero has a **shell or coral element** (Coral Knight has a coral-plate shield; Tide Mage has a conch-spiral hood; Sunshoal Archer has a starfish quiver; Kelp Warden has a giant-clam pauldron; Abyssal Wizard has an angler-lure staff)
- Every bubble is rendered as a **glassy water-sphere with a soft kelp-leaf accent** — instantly distinguishes Pop Brigade bubbles from generic match-3 bubbles
- Background environment always has a **moving school of tiny fish** in the bottom layer — the game feels alive even at rest

**HUD treatment:**
- Frame: woven-rope and bleached-driftwood borders
- Energy meter: a tide-line that rises and falls
- Gacha screen: a Tidemother shrine with a pearl-summon animation

**Why this owns visual lane:**
- No mobile TD currently uses saturated reef + bleach contrast
- The kelp-leaf-on-bubble motif is a 48px-readable signature mark
- Five-color faction palette is *the* most saturated-but-coherent palette in the casual TD shelf

## 7. App icon at 48px

A chibi Coral Knight in mid-swing pose, coral-red armor with bright kelp-green accents, holding up a glowing water-bubble that contains a tiny Sunshoal Archer silhouette. Background is a vertical split — saturated coral-pink on the left, deep-trench navy on the right — with one bleached-white silhouette creeping in from the navy side as the threat hook.

Reads as: "cute hero game, ocean setting, something is wrong." Three pieces of information at 48px.

## 9. First 30 seconds of D1 (player-voice)

The screen opens on a coral reef being eaten by a creeping white edge. A voice — the Tidemother — says *"Little weaver. We need you."* A spirit-bubble cluster forms above the reef; one bubble glows with a tiny coral-armored silhouette inside. Tutorial finger taps it: *"Free your first hero."*

I drag to aim, release. A pearl flies up, hits a three-of-red match, pops, and Coral Knight drops onto the reef floor with a *thunk*. Voice line: *"By the tide!"* The reef beneath him gains a small splash of color back. Three more hero-bubbles light up. Move counter shows **8 pearls left**.

I fire again — a 5-match this time. The freed hero drops at Silver tier, slightly bigger, full-color. The cluster sweeps away. Banner: *"The Bleach approaches."* A Pale Eel slithers down the lane.

I drag Coral Knight a step left to intercept. The eel dies. The reef gets a little more color. **Stage 1 clear.** Confetti, pearl-drop, the reef briefly blooms full saturation. Tutorial finger taps **Next Tide**.

## 10. Live-ops scaffold (what the faction system enables)

- **Banners**: Each faction has 2–3 limited heroes per year (Coral Princess, Tide Sage, Sun Hawk, Kelp Witch, Abyss Lord)
- **Realms**: Each realm is a biome (Shore → Reef → Kelp → Trench → Abyss), unlocking new enemy variants of The Bleach
- **Seasons**: Quarterly story arcs — "The Bleaching of the Kelp Forest" / "The Coral Court Falls" / "Into the Trench"
- **Events**: Faction-vs-faction tournaments (Coral Court vs Sunshoal "Sunrise Skirmish"), guild-coop boss raids on a Whitening King variant
- **Skins**: Per-faction seasonal skins (Coral Knight in Festival armor, Tide Mage in Storm-cloak, etc.) — same skeleton, different palette = cheap to produce
- **Collabs (long-term)**: This theme is collab-friendly in ways Soda/Pop Idol aren't — animal-conservation orgs, real reef nonprofits, ocean-themed brands

## 11. Open questions (what to validate before committing)

1. **Does "color restoration" feel rewarding in playtest?** The mechanic loop (pop bubble → reef regains color → wave dies → reef regains more color) needs to land in greybox. If players don't *notice* the color coming back, the emotional spine is dead.
2. **Does the 5-faction palette read at 48px on a cheap Android?** The saturated palette must survive a Tecno/Xiaomi mid-tier screen. UA creative test in PH/ID is the gate.
3. **Is "Reefweaver" a strong enough player identity?** Cleaner than "Burst Commander" but needs to land in user testing — does the player feel like a Reefweaver, or just a tap-tapper?
4. **Bleach mob roster scalability.** Five biomes × 4–6 Bleach variants each = 20–30 enemy designs. Doable for a team-of-10 if reskin-heavy, risky if every Bleach variant needs unique animation.
5. **Does the theme survive without the eco-villain crutch?** The Bleach must read as *mythic threat*, not *climate metaphor*. Easy to slip. Watch player tester language: if they say "oh like climate change" → reframe is needed.
